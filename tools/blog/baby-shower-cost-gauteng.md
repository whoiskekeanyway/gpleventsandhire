Planning a baby shower in Midrand, Johannesburg, Sandton or anywhere in Gauteng? The cost depends mostly on three things: how many guests you're inviting, how styled you want the space to look, and how much you'd like to do yourself.

This guide covers what baby shower décor costs with us in 2026, what you get at each level, the other costs to plan for, and a few easy ways to make your budget go further.

## The quick answer

A fully styled baby shower setup from GPL Events & Hire starts at **R6,500 for up to 20 guests**, **R9,500 for 20–50 guests** and **R14,500 for 50–80 guests**. Setup on the day is included in every package. Delivery and collection is charged separately — usually around **R1,600** for most Gauteng venues.

## What else to budget for

Décor is one part of the total. When you plan your budget, leave room for:

- **Venue** — nothing extra if you host at home or in a garden; a hired venue is a separate cost.
- **Food and drinks** — most hosts arrange catering, platters or a picnic-style spread separately.
- **Cake and desserts** — the cake is usually ordered from a baker; our packages include a styled table to show it off.
- **Delivery and collection** — usually around R1,600 with us for most Gauteng venues.
- **Gifts and favours** — a gift for the mom-to-be and small favours for guests if you'd like them.
- **Extras** — games, a photographer, or a guestbook.

## How to make your budget go further

- **Choose the right guest range.** Packages are priced by guest numbers, so a firm guest list helps you pick the right level from the start.
- **Host at home or in a garden.** You save on venue hire, and we bring the full setup to you.
- **Pick one statement piece.** A balloon arch with BABY marquee letters makes a bigger impact in photos than lots of small décor spread around the room.
- **Hire instead of buying.** If you're happy to set up yourself, you can hire individual items — [marquee letters](hire/marquee-letters.html), [backdrops](hire/backdrops.html), plinths and more — priced per item, per day. Delivery is quoted separately and setup isn't included with hire-only orders.
- **Book early.** We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, and 6–8 weeks for busy periods like December and school holidays.

## Hire-only or a styled package?

**Hire-only** suits smaller, relaxed showers where you're happy to collect items (or have them delivered) and set everything up yourself. You only pay for the pieces you need.

**A styled package** suits hosts who'd rather enjoy the day. We design the look in your colours and theme, deliver everything, set it up before your guests arrive and collect it afterwards.

Event Package FAQs

Common questions about our event packages

What is included in an event package?

Every package includes the décor and hire items listed on the card, styling, and setup on the day by our team. Delivery and collection are charged separately — usually around R1,600 for most Gauteng venues — and confirmed upfront in your quote.

How much is delivery and collection?

Delivery and collection is usually around R1,600 for most Gauteng venues. The exact amount depends on your venue's distance from Midrand and the size of the setup, and we confirm it in your quote before you book.

How do I secure my date?

A 70% deposit secures your date, and the balance is due a week before your event. We accept payment by EFT.

Can I customise a package to add or remove items?

Absolutely — every package is a starting point. Add extras like marquee letters, a champagne wall or more balloons, or swap items to suit your theme, and we'll tailor your quote.

Is there a security deposit?

Some items may require a refundable security deposit, but we waive it for many bookings. We'll always tell you upfront if it applies.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

With enough notice, your deposit can be refunded or moved to a new available date. Please contact us as early as possible so we can help.

How do I know which package suits my guest count?

Each package shows a guest guide. If you're between sizes, use the 'Help me choose' tool above or WhatsApp us — we can scale any package up or down.

What areas do you cover?

We style events across Midrand, Waterfall, Carlswald, Kyalami, Fourways, Sandton, Centurion and Johannesburg. For venues further out, enquire and we'll confirm whether we can accommodate you.

Planning an event?

Tell us your date and venue — we'll confirm availability and pricing, usually the same day.

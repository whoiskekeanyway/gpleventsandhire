Stands & Plinths Hire FAQs

Common questions about stand hire in Midrand

Do the flower stands come with flowers included?

No — we hire the stand only. You can arrange your own florals or we can connect you with a florist who works with our stands regularly. WhatsApp us and we'll help you find the right combination.

What heights are available?

We offer tall floral stands (sold in pairs), round pedestal stands, and cylinder stands at various heights. Heights are confirmed when you enquire — we'll match the stand to your venue ceiling height and table height.

Can I hire flower stands without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery and setup included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

Do plinths come in different sizes?

Yes — our white square plinths are available in small, medium and large. Clear acrylic plinths also come in three sizes. Pricing varies by size, so select a size when enquiring and we'll confirm availability.

How much weight can the plinths support?

Our plinths are designed to support floral arrangements, cakes, vases and decorative displays. They are not rated for heavy structural loads. If you have a specific weight requirement, let us know and we'll confirm suitability.

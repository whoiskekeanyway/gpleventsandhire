Crockery & Glassware Hire FAQs

Common questions about crockery and glassware hire in Midrand

How many glasses or plates can I hire?

Glassware and plates are hired per item, so you can book exactly the quantity you need. WhatsApp us with your quantities and we'll confirm availability for your event.

Do the glasses come washed and ready to use?

Yes — all glassware is supplied clean and event-ready. We just ask that glasses are rinsed before collection or return.

Can I hire crockery without a full event package?

Yes — all items are available for individual hire. Mix and match sets as needed with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Setup is not included in the hire price. Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your venue's location — we cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas and will confirm the cost when you enquire.

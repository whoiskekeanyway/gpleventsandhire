Crockery & Glassware Hire FAQs

Common questions about crockery and glassware hire in Midrand

How many glasses or plates can I hire?

Our glassware and charger plates are hired in sets of 10. If you need more than one set, WhatsApp us with your quantities and we'll confirm availability and pricing for your event.

Do the glasses come washed and ready to use?

Yes — all glassware is delivered clean and event-ready. We collect after the event and handle the washing, so you don't need to worry about returns.

Can I hire crockery without a full event package?

Yes — all items are available for individual hire. Mix and match sets as needed with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

Drinks Board Hire FAQs

Common questions about drinks board hire in Midrand

Can the drinks board be personalised with our own menu?

Yes — we offer custom text options for acrylic and printed drinks boards. WhatsApp us with your menu and event details and we'll advise on the best personalisation option for your chosen board.

What is the standard hire period for drinks boards?

Standard hire covers the duration of your event (typically one day). We deliver before your event begins and collect after it ends. Extended hire for multi-day events can be arranged — just let us know.

Can I hire just a drinks board without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Setup is not included in the hire price. Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your venue's location — we cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas and will confirm the cost when you enquire.

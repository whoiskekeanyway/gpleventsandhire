Marquee Letter & Number Hire FAQs

Common questions about marquee letter and number hire in Midrand

Can I hire specific letters or numbers for my event?

Yes — we hire individual letters (A–Z) and numbers (0–9), so you can spell out any name, word, or number for your event. WhatsApp us with what you need and we'll confirm availability.

Are the marquee letters LED-lit?

Yes — our marquee letters and numbers are illuminated with LED lighting. They work for both indoor and outdoor events and require a standard power point nearby.

Do you have popular sets like 21, LOVE or BABY ready to hire?

Yes — popular sets include LOVE, BABY, MR & MRS and Marry Me, and milestone numbers like 21 or 18 can be made from our marquee numbers. These are great for milestone birthdays and baby showers. Enquire early as sets get booked out for peak dates.

Is delivery included in the marquee letter hire price?

Setup is not included in the hire price. Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your venue's location — we cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas and will confirm the cost when you enquire.

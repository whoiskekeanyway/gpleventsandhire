Lawn Game Hire FAQs

Common questions about lawn game hire in Midrand

Are the lawn games suitable for children as well as adults?

Yes — Giant Jenga, Cornhole, and Ring Toss are suitable for all ages. Adult supervision is recommended for younger children, particularly with Giant Jenga blocks. The games are a favourite addition to family events.

Can the lawn games be used indoors?

Yes — all three games can be used indoors on hard floors or carpeted surfaces as well as outdoors on grass or paving. We'll advise on spacing requirements when you confirm your venue.

Can I hire a single game without a full package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

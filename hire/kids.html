Our kids chairs and tables are sized for children aged approximately 2–10 years. WhatsApp us with the ages of the children attending and we'll confirm the best fit.

Each setup includes kids chairs and a kids table arranged and ready for your party. WhatsApp us for full details on what's included and any styling add-ons available.

Backdrop Hire FAQs

Common questions about backdrop hire in Midrand

How are the backdrops installed — do they need to be fixed to a wall?

Our backdrops use free-standing frames that require no wall fixings and cause no damage. They can be set up on any flat surface, indoors or in a marquee, without specialist installation.

Can backdrops be used outdoors?

Free-standing backdrops can be used in covered outdoor areas such as marquees or patios. We don't recommend exposed outdoor use in windy conditions — let us know your venue and we'll advise on the best option.

Can I hire a single backdrop without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery and setup included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

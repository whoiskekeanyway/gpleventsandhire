Audio Guestbook FAQs

Common questions about audio guestbook hire in Midrand

What is an audio guestbook and how does it work?

An audio guestbook allows your guests to record a personal voice message using a retro telephone or digital station — creating a lasting audio memory you can replay for years. Guests simply pick up the handset and speak when prompted.

Is someone on-site to manage the audio guestbook during the event?

Yes — we set up and fully test the station before your event begins. We can also provide an attendant to assist guests and ensure messages are captured correctly. Confirm when you enquire.

Can I hire the audio guestbook without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead to secure availability, especially for weekends and peak seasons. Last-minute enquiries are always welcome — WhatsApp us and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

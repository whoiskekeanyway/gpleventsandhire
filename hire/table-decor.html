Table Décor & Accessories Hire FAQs

Common questions about table décor and accessories hire in Midrand

Can table décor be matched to our event colour scheme?

Yes — we're happy to advise on combinations that complement your colours and theme. WhatsApp us with your colour palette and event style and we'll suggest the most suitable centrepieces and décor pieces from our catalogue.

How many centrepieces do I need per table?

One centrepiece per table is standard. For long banquet tables, two or three centrepieces spaced evenly creates a fuller look. We can help you estimate quantities based on your table layout.

Can I hire individual décor items without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

Arch Hire FAQs

Common questions about arch hire in Midrand

Can the arch be decorated with florals, balloons, or fabric?

Yes — our arches are designed to be dressed with any decoration. Whether you're adding a balloon garland, floral arrangement, fabric draping, or greenery, the structure provides a solid base to work with.

Do you assemble and remove the arch on the day?

Yes — professional setup and takedown are included in the hire price. We'll install the arch before your event and collect it once it ends, so you have nothing to worry about on the day.

Can I hire just an arch without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery and setup included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

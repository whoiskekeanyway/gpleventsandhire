Are flowers and vinyl included in the R300 hire price?

No — the R300 hire price covers the box frame only. Flowers and vinyl text are not included and are quoted separately. The fully dressed Welcome Board (flowers + vinyl + frame) is available from R980. WhatsApp us to enquire about the full package.

Can I hire the flower box without a full event package?

Yes — all items are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery and collection included in the price?

Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your location. You're also welcome to collect from our warehouse free of charge — by appointment only. Simply let us know your preference when requesting your quote.

Audio Guestbook Hire FAQs

Common questions about audio guestbook hire in Midrand

How much does it cost to hire the audio guestbook?

The retro telephone audio guestbook is R2,500 per day. That includes the guestbook, an A5 acrylic instruction stand, an A2 correx sign board and an easel stand. Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your venue's location.

How do guests use the audio guestbook?

Guests simply pick up the handset, wait for the short prompt, and leave their voice message. The instruction stand explains each step, so it's easy for everyone — grandparents included.

Are the recordings saved?

Yes — all voice messages are saved digitally and provided to you after the event as audio files, so you can listen back to your guests' messages for years to come.

Is setup included?

Setup isn't included in the hire price. The guestbook is simple to place on a table with its instruction stand and sign board — ask us if you'd like help on the day.

Is delivery included?

Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your venue's location.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible — weekends and wedding season book up quickly. WhatsApp us your date to check availability.

Marquee Letters Hire FAQs

Common questions about marquee letter hire in Midrand

How much does marquee letter hire cost?

Marquee letters are R300 per letter — for example, a 4-letter word like LOVE is R1,200. Delivery and collection are quoted separately.

How do I know which letters are available?

WhatsApp us with the letters you need (e.g. a name or word) and we'll confirm availability for your event date.

Do the marquee letters require a stand?

The letters are freestanding and can be placed on the floor or on a table or plinth. No additional stand is required.

Do the letters need power?

Yes — marquee letters need a standard power outlet. We recommend confirming your venue has accessible power near your display area.

Can I combine letters and numbers?

Yes — mix letters (R300 each) with marquee numbers (R350 each), for example a name and an age.

Is delivery included?

Delivery and collection are quoted separately. Free warehouse collection is available by appointment.

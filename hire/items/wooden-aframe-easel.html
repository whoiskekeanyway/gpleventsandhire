Wooden A-Frame Easel Hire FAQs

Common questions about easel hire in Midrand

What size board fits the wooden A-frame easel?

The wooden A-frame easel is designed to hold A1-size boards perfectly — ideal for our Correx welcome boards and most standard signage. If you have a non-standard board size, WhatsApp us and we'll confirm suitability.

Can I hire the easel without a welcome board?

Yes — all items are available for individual hire. The easel can be hired on its own if you have your own board or signage to display.

Is delivery and collection included in the price?

Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your location. You're also welcome to collect from our warehouse free of charge — by appointment only. Simply let us know your preference when requesting your quote.

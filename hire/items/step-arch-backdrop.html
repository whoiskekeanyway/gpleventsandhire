Step Arch Backdrop Hire FAQs

Common questions about the step arch backdrop hire in Midrand

How much does the step arch backdrop cost to hire?

The step arch backdrop is R1,200 per day. Decoration is quoted separately, and delivery and collection are quoted based on your location.

What are the dimensions of the step arch backdrop?

The step arch stands approximately 2m tall and 1.8m wide — large enough to frame a couple, a cake table or a full photo setup comfortably.

Can the step arch be decorated with florals or balloons?

Yes — the tiered frame is designed to be dressed with balloon garlands, floral arrangements, greenery or fabric draping. It also looks striking left bare.

Does it work with marquee letters?

Yes — marquee letters or numbers in front of the step arch make a popular photo backdrop for birthdays and weddings.

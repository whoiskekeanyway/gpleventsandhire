Champagne Wall

An elegant white champagne wall built to hold glasses in a clean, eye-catching display that doubles as both a serving station and a statement décor piece. Standing 2.1 metres tall and 90cm wide, this display rack is designed to present champagne and prosecco glasses securely across multiple tiers — giving guests a self-serve experience that feels effortlessly luxurious. Available in two silhouettes: a clean-lined square top or a softer arch top to suit your event style. Glasses can be supplied at an additional cost for a fully dressed setup.

Champagne Wall Hire FAQs

Common questions about champagne wall hire in Midrand

How much does the champagne wall cost to hire?

The champagne wall is R950 per day in either square top or arch top. Glasses are extra, and delivery and collection are quoted separately.

What are the dimensions of the champagne wall?

The champagne wall stands approximately 2.1m tall and 90cm wide — tall enough to make a statement while fitting comfortably into most venue spaces.

Are champagne glasses included in the hire price?

Glasses are not included in the standard R950 hire price but can be added at an additional cost. WhatsApp us and we'll quote for the wall with glasses included.

What is the difference between the square top and arch top?

Both versions are the same size and hold the same number of glasses — the only difference is the silhouette. The square top suits modern, minimalist styling; the arch top feels softer and more romantic.

Is delivery and collection included in the price?

Delivery and collection are quoted separately based on your location. You're also welcome to collect from our warehouse free of charge — by appointment only.

Sign Hire FAQs

Common questions about sign hire in Midrand

Can signs be personalised with our names, dates, or event details?

Yes — custom text options are available for LED neon signs, acrylic table numbers, and directional signs. WhatsApp us with your event details and we'll advise on the best personalisation option for each sign type.

Are LED neon signs plug-in or battery-powered?

Our LED neon signs are plug-in and require a standard power point nearby. Please ensure your venue has accessible power points in the areas where you plan to display the signs.

Can I hire individual signs without a full event package?

Yes — all items in this category are available for individual hire. Mix and match as many pieces as your event needs, with no requirement to book a full styling package.

Is delivery included in the hire price?

Delivery, setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

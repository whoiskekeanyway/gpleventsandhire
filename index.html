Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking with us

How much does event planning cost in Midrand?

Our event packages start from R2,500 for basic setups, with full luxury packages from R9,000+. Individual décor hire starts from R150 per item. Every event is unique — contact us for a personalised quote tailored to your vision and budget.

Do you deliver and set up at my venue?

Yes — delivery, professional setup, and post-event collection are all included in our packages. We serve Midrand and the surrounding Gauteng areas, including Johannesburg, Sandton, Centurion, Waterfall, Carlswald, and Fourways.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date. For peak seasons (December, Valentine's Day, school holidays), 6–8 weeks ahead is advisable. Last-minute bookings may be possible — contact us to check availability.

Do you offer full packages or can I hire individual items?

Both! You can book one of our all-inclusive event packages or hire individual items — backdrops, marquee letters, welcome boards, easel stands, and more. Browse our hire catalogue for standalone options.

Do you work weekends and public holidays?

Yes — we work 7 days a week including weekends and public holidays, 08:00–20:00. Weekend and peak-season slots fill up fast, so early booking is always recommended.

Planning an event?

Tell us your date and venue — we'll confirm availability and pricing, usually the same day.

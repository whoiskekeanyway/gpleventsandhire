12 Year-End Function Ideas for Johannesburg Offices (2026)

11 October 2026 · 6 min read · By GPL Events & Hire

The year-end function is the one event your whole team remembers. It's a thank-you for a long year, a chance to celebrate wins, and — if clients are invited — a reflection of your brand.

Here are 12 ideas we use for year-end functions across Johannesburg, Sandton, Midrand and Centurion, from simple touches for an office celebration to a fully styled venue. We've added what things cost and when to book, so you can plan with confidence.

1. Roll out a VIP red carpet entrance

Nothing says "you're celebrated" like a red carpet with gold stanchions at the door. It sets the tone the moment your team arrives — and it's the photo everyone posts. A red carpet with gold stanchions is included in our Signature corporate package, or you can hire them on their own.

2. Welcome guests with a branded welcome board

A personalised welcome board with your company name, logo and the event title makes even a familiar office or restaurant feel like an occasion. Every one of our corporate packages includes a branded welcome board on an easel.

3. Put your initials — or the year — in lights

Light-up marquee letters are a year-end favourite. Spell out your company initials, a team name or "2026" and you have an instant photo spot. Marquee letters start from R300 per letter and marquee numbers from R350 each. Our Luxe package includes up to four letters.

4. Serve welcome drinks from a champagne wall

A champagne wall turns welcome drinks into a moment. Guests take a glass as they walk in, and it doubles as décor all evening. We styled a branded champagne wall for the Equinix JNO office opening in Johannesburg, right beside the badge drop station. Champagne wall hire is R950 per day.

5. Use your brand colours in a balloon arch

A balloon arch or garland in your exact brand colours is one of the most affordable ways to make a venue feel on-brand. It frames the entrance, the stage or the photo wall. Our Signature corporate package includes a balloon arch in your brand colours.

6. Create a photo backdrop for team and award photos

A clean, styled backdrop gives you professional-looking team photos and award shots — content your marketing and HR teams can use all year. A panel photo backdrop is included in every corporate package, and you can browse more backdrops to hire.

7. Style the tables, not just the entrance

Guests spend most of the evening seated, so table styling matters. Coordinated centrepieces, quality crockery and glassware and a consistent colour palette make a hired venue feel premium. Our Signature package includes table décor for up to 12 tables.

8. Pick a theme your team will love

A theme gives everyone something to dress up for. Popular choices for year-end functions include:

  • Black and gold — elegant and easy to brand.
  • All-white — clean, modern and great in photos.
  • Festive with a twist — seasonal touches in your brand colours rather than red and green.
  • Awards night — a stage area, backdrop and a red carpet for winners.

9. Collect messages with an audio guestbook

Instead of a card, let your team leave voice messages on a retro telephone — perfect for a farewell, a retirement or simply a record of the year. Our audio guestbook comes with an instruction stand, a welcome board and an easel, from R2,500 per day.

10. Play games at a daytime or outdoor function

Hosting a lunchtime function or a garden celebration? Giant Jenga and giant tic-tac-toe get people mixing across departments. Each is R250 per day.

11. Send everyone home with a gift

A thoughtful gift for staff or clients ends the year on a high. Our corporate gifts start from R1,500 with no minimum order, logo branding on packaging, ribbon and cards, and bulk-order discounts. Gift delivery is free in Midrand, Waterfall, Carlswald, Kyalami and Fourways, and R150 elsewhere in Gauteng.

12. Let a styling team handle the setup

Your team should enjoy the function — not spend the afternoon blowing up balloons. With a styled package, we design the look, deliver everything, set up before your guests arrive and collect afterwards.

When to book your year-end function

November and December book up fast. As a guide:

  • October to early November — the best time to enquire for a December function.
  • Two to four weeks ahead — our usual recommendation outside the busy season (6–8 weeks for December).
  • A week before — the balance is due. Your date is secured when you pay the 70% deposit by EFT.

Your year-end planning checklist

  1. Confirm your date, venue and guest count.
  2. Ask the venue about access times and the setup window — we schedule our team around it.
  3. Send us your logo and brand colours for welcome boards and balloons.
  4. Choose a package or add-ons, and decide on gifts for staff or clients.
  5. Secure your date with the 70% deposit.

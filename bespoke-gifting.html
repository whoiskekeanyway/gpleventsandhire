Bespoke Gifting FAQs

Everything you need to know about our curated gift packages

Can I fully customise the contents of a gift hamper?

Yes — all our hampers are completely customisable. Tell us the recipient, the occasion, your budget, and any preferences (dietary requirements, preferred brands, colour palette) and we'll curate something truly unique for them.

Do you offer bulk ordering for corporate gifts?

Yes — bulk and corporate orders are welcome, with discounts available for larger quantities. We offer branded packaging, custom message cards, and Gauteng-wide delivery. Contact us with your quantity and requirements for a corporate gifting quote.

What is the lead time for a bespoke gift order?

Standard orders require 3–5 business days. For larger or highly customised orders, we may need 7–10 business days. For urgent requests, WhatsApp us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate your timeline.

Do you deliver gifts, or is it collection only?

We offer delivery across Gauteng. Delivery cost is based on distance. Collection from Midrand is also available if you prefer. Confirm your delivery area and preferred date when placing your order.

Can I add a personalised message or corporate branding?

Yes — all hampers include a personalised message or card at no extra charge. For corporate branding such as logos on packaging, tissue paper, or ribbon, let us know your requirements and we'll advise on the available options and any associated costs.

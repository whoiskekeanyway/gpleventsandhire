Bespoke Gifting FAQs

Everything you need to know about our curated gift packages

Can I fully customise the contents of a gift hamper?

Yes — all our hampers are completely customisable. Tell us the recipient, the occasion, your budget and any preferences (dietary requirements, favourite brands, colours) and we'll curate something unique.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free in Midrand, Waterfall, Carlswald, Kyalami and Fourways. Anywhere else in Gauteng is a R150 flat rate. Collection from Midrand is also available.

How do I pay?

Full payment by EFT confirms your order, including the delivery fee where applicable.

What is the lead time for a gift order?

Standard orders take 3–5 business days. Larger or highly customised orders may need 7–10 business days. Flower bouquets can be delivered the same day when ordered before 12:00.

Are the flowers fresh?

Yes — every bouquet is made with fresh flowers. Order before 12:00 for same-day delivery.

Do you do corporate gifting?

Yes — there's no minimum order, bulk-order discounts are available, and we can add your logo to packaging, ribbon and cards. Use the corporate form on this page or WhatsApp us your quantities.

Can I add a personalised message?

Yes — every gift includes a personalised message card at no extra charge.

Planning an event?

Tell us your date and venue — we'll confirm availability and pricing, usually the same day.

Hire Items FAQs

Common questions about our décor hire catalogue

Can I hire individual items without booking a full package?

Yes — any item in our catalogue can be hired individually. Popular standalone hires include round arch backdrops, panel backdrop sets, acrylic welcome boards, marquee letters, and easel stands. Mix and match as many items as your event needs.

What is the standard hire period?

Standard hire covers the duration of your event — typically one day. We deliver and set up before your event begins, and collect after it ends. If you need items for multiple days, let us know when you enquire and we'll arrange extended hire.

Is delivery and collection included in hire prices?

Delivery, professional setup, and collection are included. We cover Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas. Delivery cost may vary based on distance from Midrand — we'll confirm this clearly when you enquire.

Can I view the items before committing to a hire?

Yes — WhatsApp us to arrange a viewing. We're happy to show you items and discuss styling options before you commit. We can also share photos of setups we've done using the same items.

Can hire items be styled alongside my own décor?

Absolutely — our items are designed to complement any styling. Arch backdrops pair beautifully with balloon garlands or florals, and marquee letters work with any theme. We're happy to advise on combinations that work well together for your event.
